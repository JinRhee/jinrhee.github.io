Robotics engineer working to make robots smarter at [Riibotics](https://www.riibotics.io). My interests lie in perception and spatial intelligence for mobile robots.

I previously read Engineering Science at Oxford where I did research on deep-learning based odometry and SLAM at the [Oxford Robotics Institute](https://dynamic.robots.ox.ac.uk). I have worked at labs and startups in Daejeon, Seoul, and London. I am open to interesting collaboration opportunities; please reach out to me on any of my socials.
