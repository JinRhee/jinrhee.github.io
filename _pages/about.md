Robotics engineer working on *visual-inertial odometry* and SLAM: helping robots know where they are.

I studied Engineering Science at [Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk) (St John's College). Before that I was a visiting researcher at [KAIST Urban Robotics Lab](https://url.kaist.ac.kr) and a mechatronics intern at [Bear Robotics](https://www.bearrobotics.ai). I also co-lead [Qronk](https://github.com/JinRhee), a low-cost 3D-printed quadruped.
